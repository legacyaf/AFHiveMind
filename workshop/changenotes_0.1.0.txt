[h2]0.1.0 - first release[/h2]
[list]
[*]Learns from player-versus-bot fights and adjusts one difficulty figure every 10 minutes towards a target player win rate (80% by default). Old fights fade with a 72-hour half-life.
[*]Tunes bot accuracy, damage dealt and detection range at spawn, with extra adjustments for hot zones and for players near the spawn.
[*]Range counter: against snipers bots flank wider and some carry scoped rifles; against rushers they push in and some carry automatics. Bots keep their own clothes and gear.
[*]Tunes bots that other mods create directly from Expansion AI (convoys, helicopter and tank patrols). Bots following a player are left alone.
[*]Admin F9 debug panel, plus brain.json, report.txt and hivemind.log in the server profile.
[*]Every limit is a setting in config.json, re-read every 10 minutes.
[/list]
