[h1]AF HiveMind - AI that learns how you fight[/h1]

HiveMind sits on top of [b]DayZ Expansion AI[/b]. It watches every fight between players and AI bots, keeps a memory of how those fights go, and tunes newly spawned bots from what it has learned. If players farm the bots, they get sharper. If the bots dominate, they ease off. The goal: fights against AI stay challenging but winnable, whether your server is full of veterans or fresh spawns.

No wallhacks, no aimbots, no knowledge of where players are. HiveMind turns the same dials an admin would turn by hand, only it keeps doing it, based on how your community actually plays.

[h2]How it learns[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Watches player-versus-bot fights only[/b]: who won, from how far, with which weapon, where on the map and at what hour. Player-versus-player and bot-versus-bot fights are ignored.
[*][b]Every 10 minutes[/b] it compares the share of fights players win with a target (80% by default) and nudges one difficulty figure between 0 and 1. At 0.5, bots are exactly as you configured them.
[*][b]It forgets slowly.[/b] Old fights fade (half-life 72 hours, downtime included), so the bots follow how people play now, not last month.
[*]Until it has seen a few fights, it changes nothing.
[/list]

[h2]What it changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Accuracy[/b]: from 60% to 150% of what you configured.
[*][b]Damage dealt[/b]: from 75% to 130% of what you configured.
[*][b]Sight and hearing[/b]: how far bots notice you (75% to 110%, never past about 1.1 km) and how far they come to check a gunshot (70% to 130%).
[*][b]Hot zones[/b]: in an area players farm, bots get a little tougher and patrols come out bigger (up to half as many bots again, at most 3 extra). Areas where bots dominate ease off.
[*][b]Range counter[/b]: if your players mostly snipe, bots flank wider and up to 40% of new patrol bots carry scoped rifles. Snipers are also hunted down from further away. If they mostly rush, bots push straight in and up to 40% carry automatics. Bots keep their faction's clothes and gear; only the weapon changes.
[*][b]Night counter[/b]: bots without night vision are nearly blind at night. If many fights happen after dark, up to half of new patrol bots get NVGs and switch them on at dusk. The goggles are removed when the bot dies, so they never become loot.
[*][b]Bounty loot[/b]: harder bots may drop one extra item when a player kills them (up to 75% chance), from a common list (medical, ammo, magazines) or a rare one (NVG, rangefinder, scopes, plate carrier). Tougher bots, better rewards.
[/list]
Every one of these limits is a setting in config.json.

[h2]Which bots[/h2]
[list]
[*]Expansion patrols, object patrols and quest bots
[*]Bots other mods create directly from Expansion AI, such as convoy, helicopter and tank patrol mods
[*][b]Not[/b] bots a player has recruited to follow them
[/list]

[h2]For admins[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]F9 debug panel[/b] (admins only, listed by Steam64 id): current difficulty, what it has learned in plain words, and the last patrols it tuned with the reason for each number.
[*][b]Full guide for server owners[/b] (install, settings, troubleshooting): https://github.com/legacyaf/AFHiveMind/blob/main/docs/SERVER_GUIDE.md
[*][b]F10 admin controls[/b]: freeze learning, pin the difficulty, run a learning step now, or reset the brain, all in game.
[*][b]Follows the community, not one player[/b]: no single player's fights can set the difficulty alone.
[*][b]Readable files[/b] in the server profile, folder AFHiveMind: brain.json (everything it learned), report.txt (the same in plain English), hivemind.log (every fight it saw and every change of mind), config.json (all settings).
[*]config.json is re-read every 10 minutes: change settings without a restart. Settings added by an update appear in it automatically. Set "Enabled" to false to switch it off.
[*]Delete brain.json to start over from "as configured". A daily backup is kept in AFHiveMind\backups (last 7).
[*]Writes two Expansion loadouts (vanilla weapons only) for the range counter: AFHM_MarksmanWeapons and AFHM_AssaultWeapons. Edit them like any Expansion loadout.
[/list]

[h2]Installing[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Server only:[/b] load @AFHiveMind with -serverMod=. Players download nothing. Use report.txt and hivemind.log instead of the panel.
[*][b]With the F9 panel:[/b] load it with -mod= on server and clients, and copy the key from Keys to your server's keys folder.
[*]Start the server once to create AFHiveMind\config.json in the server profile, then add your Steam64 id under "Admins" to use the panel.
[/list]

[h2]Player data[/h2]
For each player who fights bots, HiveMind stores their name, Steam ID, bots killed, deaths to bots and average kill distance, in a file on the server only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Entries fade and are removed when a player stops fighting bots.
